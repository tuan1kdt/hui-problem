\subsection{Analysis}

\paragraph{TKU} is the slowest due to Phase~2 re-scanning the database for every candidate. Its memory is low at small $k$ (Mushroom $k{=}1$: 7.93\,MB vs.\ TKO's 19.45\,MB) thanks to UP-Tree compression, but grows sharply as $k$ increases (Retail $k{=}10$: 1739\,MB).

\paragraph{TKO} eliminates Phase~2 via utility-list joins and maintains stable memory on sparse data (Retail: 74--84\,MB across all $k$). However, it starts with a low $minUtility$, and on very dense datasets its utility-lists may explode in size.

\paragraph{THUI} achieves the best runtime by raising $minUtility$ aggressively \textit{before} mining (Retail $k{=}1000$: 10.3\,s vs.\ 183\,s TKO, 192\,s TKU). The trade-off is memory: it grows from 19\,MB to 2911\,MB on Retail as $k$ increases from 1 to 1000 — 34$\times$ higher than TKO.
