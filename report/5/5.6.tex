\subsection{Parallelization Experiments}
We setup experiments to evaluate the parallel algorithms compared to their sequential counterparts. We implemented parallel versions of TKU, TKO and THUI using Go's concurrency primitives and MapReduce where applicable. We ran these implementations on a multi-core machine with 6 cores and 12 logical processors, using synthetic datasets of varying sizes to assess scalability and performance.
